    background: white
    richText: >
      <p>The best immersive installations aren't technology bolted onto a
      finished space — they're shaped by the technology from the start. We get
      involved early, working alongside exhibit designers, AV integrators, and
      creative teams to make sure the software, the hardware, and the physical
      environment are all designed together.</p>

      <p>We bring deep experience in how people actually move through and
      interact with immersive spaces — what draws them in, what sustains their
      attention, and what makes an experience accessible to every person in the
      room. ADA compliance isn't a final checklist item; it's a design
      consideration we build in from the beginning. Repositionable interfaces,
      screen reader support, interaction models that work for visitors of all
      ages and comfort levels with technology.</p>

      <p>We also know that a finished installation is just the beginning. We
      build with maintainability in mind and stay available long after opening
      day.</p>
