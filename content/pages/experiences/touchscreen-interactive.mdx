    background: white
    richText: >
      <p>We're a software-first team, which means we think about the interaction
      before we think about the screen. But great touchscreen experiences aren't
      just about clean code — they require a deep understanding of how people
      actually engage with technology in public spaces.</p>

      <p>We design for the person standing in front of the screen: their
      curiosity, their attention span, and their comfort with technology. That
      means interfaces that are immediately legible to a first-time visitor and
      still engaging to someone who lingers. It also means building with
      accessibility in mind from the start — because in museums, visitor
      centers, and public spaces, a great experience should be available to
      everyone. We've built ADA-compliant interactives with screen reader
      support and wheelchair-friendly interaction models, and we treat that work
      as a design challenge, not an afterthought.</p>

      <p>We also serve as a development and technical partner for AV integrators
      and creative agencies who need a trusted team to build the software
      layer.</p>
