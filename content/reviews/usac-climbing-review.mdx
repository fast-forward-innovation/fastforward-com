  # Section 3: What we found
  - type: Prose
    heading: What we found
    html: >
      <p>About 20 platforms and vendors sit behind the member experience.
      Sport80 is the system of record for membership, compliance and
      payments; Vertical-Life handles competition entries and results; the
      Education Hub runs on Moodle Workplace; and the website ties them
      together with links.</p>

      <p>Much of what members need to know lives outside the site. Season
      guides, schedules, qualified-athlete lists and officials lists are
      published Google Docs and Sheets, and many requests run through Google
      Forms. That content is hard to find, can't be searched from the site,
      and sits outside USA Climbing's design.</p>

      <p>The biggest friction is at the seams. Competitors and parents join
      in Sport80, then re-enter the same details in Vertical-Life to
      register. When the details don't match exactly, the error doesn't say
      which field is wrong.</p>

  # Section 7: A phased path
  - type: Prose
    heading: A phased path
    html: >
      <p>Each step stands on its own and sets up the next, so USA Climbing
      can commit one phase at a time.</p>

      <ol>
        <li><strong>Discovery and fractional CTO.</strong> We audit the
        platforms, interview staff, map journeys with real members, and
        deliver a costed roadmap the board can act on. A fractional CTO
        retainer gives USA Climbing senior technology leadership for vendor
        decisions and contracts. About 9–11 weeks.</li>
        <li><strong>A new front door.</strong> We rebuild usaclimbing.org on a
        headless front end with an integration layer for single sign-on,
        Sport80 and Vertical-Life data, Education Hub launches and Google Docs
        content. About six months, and it can be split across two budget
        years.</li>
        <li><strong>Vendor consolidation.</strong> When contracts come up for
        renewal, we run requirements, demos and selection, then plan and
        support the migration. Because the site talks to vendors through the
        integration layer, a vendor change doesn't mean another redesign.
        About 14–16 weeks.</li>
      </ol>
